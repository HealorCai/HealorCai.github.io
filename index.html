During my undergraduate studies, I worked as a research intern at InternRobotics under the supervision of Dr. Jiangmiao Pang.

Selected Publications

My research focuses on Embodied AI and robotic manipulation. Representative work is highlighted below.

InternData-A1: Pioneering High-Fidelity Synthetic Data for Pre-training Generalist Policy

Yang Tian*, Yuyin Yang*, Yiman Xie*, Zetao Cai*, Xu Shi*, Ning Gao, Hangxu Liu, Xuekun Jiang, Zherui Qiu, Feng Yuan, Yaping Li, Ping Wang, Junhao Cai, Jia Zeng†, Hao Dong†, Jiangmiao Pang†

IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR), 2026

project page / paper / dataset

We introduce InternData-A1, a high-fidelity synthetic dataset with over 630k trajectories and 7,433 hours. It provides the first evidence that synthetic data alone can match the strongest π-dataset for VLA pre-training.

Gripper Keypose and Object Pointflow as Interfaces for Bimanual Robotic Manipulation

Yuyin Yang*, Zetao Cai*, Yang Tian, Jia Zeng, Jiangmiao Pang

Robotics: Science and Systems (RSS), 2025

project page / paper

We introduce gripper keypose and object pointflow as compact, generalizable interfaces for learning coordinated bimanual manipulation.

* Equal contribution. † Corresponding author.
